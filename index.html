About

I came to the University of Delaware in 2023 after an M.A. in Education and Educational Administration and a B.A. in Education at Hanyang University in Seoul, where I studied teacher collaboration, participatory school climate, and professional learning across OECD systems.

My dissertation examines Title II, Part A, the federal government's principal investment in educator quality. It pairs national allocation and expenditure data with a mixed-methods study of Delaware districts, in partnership with the Delaware Department of Education.

I expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027 and I am on the 2026 to 2027 academic job market.

Research

i.

Federal funding and district decision-making on educators and leadership

Governments invest heavily in educators and school leaders, and how that money is distributed and implemented determines what it actually buys.

  • Partners: Delaware Department of Education, CDDA
  • Published in Educational Policy (2026)
  • Presented at AEFP, SREE, UCEA, APPAM

ii.

The educator and leadership labor market

Sustaining a high-quality educator and leader workforce is essential, and the opportunities that lead there are not accessible to everyone.

  • Partner: National Association of Independent Schools
  • Working paper with preprint, co-first authorship
  • Scoping review of career metaphors in K–12 leadership
  • Presented at AERA, UCEA, NAIS Annual Conference

iii.

Professional learning and its effects on teaching and student outcomes

Ongoing learning for educators and school leaders is central to improving the quality of their practice.

  • Funders: Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation ($9.1M and $1.5M), UD Research Foundation, Spencer
  • Collaborator: Mathematica
  • Manuscripts under review at Journal of Educational Change and International Journal of Leadership in Education
