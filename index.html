I study how education policy shapes the educator and school leadership workforce. I pair national and state administrative data with interviews that explain what the numbers leave out.

Education

  • 2023 – 2027 Ph.D., Education and Social Policy, University of Delaware Advised by Dr. Lauren Bailes, with Dr. Anamarie Whitaker My dissertation, Leadership Under Constraint: Organizational Responses to Federal Investment Policy and Equity in Leadership Representation, examines Title II, Part A, the federal government's principal investment in educator quality. It pairs national allocation and expenditure data with a mixed-methods study of Delaware districts, in partnership with the Delaware Department of Education.
