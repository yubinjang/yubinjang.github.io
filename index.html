I study how education policy shapes the educator and school leadership workforce. I pair national and state administrative data with interviews that explain what the numbers leave out.

Education

  • 2023 – 2027 Ph.D., Education and Social Policy, University of Delaware Advised by Dr. Lauren Bailes, with Dr. Anamarie Whitaker My dissertation, Leadership Under Constraint: Organizational Responses to Federal Investment Policy and Equity in Leadership Representation, examines Title II, Part A, the federal government's principal investment in educator quality. It pairs national allocation and expenditure data with a mixed-methods study of Delaware districts, in partnership with the Delaware Department of Education. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027 and I am on the 2026 to 2027 academic job market.

Research

Federal funding and district decision-making on educators and leadership

Funding for educators and school leaders is limited and unevenly distributed, and how it is allocated and implemented determines what it can actually do.

With the Delaware Department of Education and CDDA. Published in Educational Policy (2026). Presented at AEFP, SREE, UCEA, and APPAM.

The educator and leadership labor market

Sustaining a high-quality educator and leader workforce is essential, and the opportunities that lead there are not accessible to everyone.

With the National Association of Independent Schools. Working paper with preprint, co-first authorship, and a scoping review of career metaphors in K–12 leadership. Presented at AERA, UCEA, and the NAIS Annual Conference.

Professional learning and its effects on teaching and student outcomes

Ongoing learning for educators and school leaders is central to improving the quality of their practice.

Funded by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation ($9.1M and $1.5M), the UD Research Foundation, and Spencer, with Mathematica. Manuscripts under review at the Journal of Mathematical Behavior and the International Journal of Leadership in Education.
