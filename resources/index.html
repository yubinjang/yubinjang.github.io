Resources

A working list of what I return to and what I send to other graduate students. Most of it is free, most of it is what I wish someone had handed me earlier, and all of it is here because I have actually used it. Everything is maintained by the people credited beside it, so links move and courses change. Tell me if one has gone stale.

Academic life

Advice on graduate school and the faculty job market, from people who have written it down so the rest of us do not have to guess.

  • 모르는 채로 나아가기 Dr. Jina Lee, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. An archive of notes on moving through graduate school without knowing where it goes.
  • Some basic tips about the academic job market Dr. Paul Bruno, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
  • Job market materials, with samples Research statements and application materials from Penn Career Services, the Cornell Graduate School, and the UCEA handbook
  • Graduate student professional development resources Dr. Sarah Bruch, University of Delaware
  • The Professor Is In, Pearls of Wisdom Dr. Karen Kelsky. Blunt advice on the market, the CV, and the letter.

Methods

Courses, primers, and textbooks that are free to read, from the probability sequence through the estimators I actually use.

Foundations

  • Statistics 110, Probability Dr. Joseph Blitzstein, Harvard University. Full lecture videos, problem sets, and the book.
  • jbstatistics Dr. Jeremy Balka. Short videos on the introductory sequence, one concept at a time.

Multilevel and longitudinal models

  • The Hitchhiker's Guide to Longitudinal Models Dr. Ethan McCormick
  • Centre for Multilevel Modelling, video course University of Bristol
  • Introduction to Multilevel Modeling Mairead Shaw and Dr. Jessica Flake, McGill University
  • Multilevel Modeling in R Richard Blissett, with UCLA IDRE

Causal inference

  • Causal Inference, The Mixtape Dr. Scott Cunningham. Free online edition with Stata, R, and Python code.
  • Causal Inference for the Brave and True Matheus Facure
  • Difference-in-differences in Stata and R Dr. Asjad Naqvi. A running catalogue of the new estimators with worked code.
  • Playing with Stata, applied econometrics Dr. Fernando Rios-Avila
  • STA 640, Causal Inference Dr. Fan Li, Duke University. Course slides and reading list.

Software

Manuals, cheat sheets, and training for the packages that do the work.

R

  • R for Data Science Hadley Wickham, Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel, and Garrett Grolemund. Second edition, free online.
  • An Introduction to R R Core Team. The official manual.
  • Just Enough R Dr. Ben Whalley. Written for people who need the analysis, not the language.
  • R Graphics Cookbook Winston Chang. Second edition, free online.
  • Discovering Statistics Using R, companion site Andy Field, Jeremy Miles, and Zoë Field. Datasets, R scripts, and self-test material from SAGE.

Stata

  • Past webinar recordings StataCorp. Free to watch.
  • Upcoming webinars StataCorp. Registration, some sessions paid.
  • Stata cheat sheets Tim Essam and Laura Hughes, hosted by StataCorp
  • Getting Started in Stata (PDF) Oscar Torres-Reyna, Princeton University

Other packages

  • Mplus training courses Muthén & Muthén. Short course schedule and archived materials.
  • Comparing qualitative analysis software CAQDAS Networking Project, University of Surrey. Side by side reviews of NVivo, ATLAS.ti, MAXQDA, and others.

Data

Public archives and portals for education and social science research, with the documentation that makes them usable.

  • Education Data Portal Urban Institute. Common Core of Data, CRDC, IPEDS, and more, harmonized and served through an API.
  • ICPSR University of Michigan. Social science data archive and summer program.
  • PISA data and methodology OECD. Every cycle, with the technical reports that explain the weights.
  • List of state administrative data A shared spreadsheet of state longitudinal data systems and access procedures.

Community and consulting

Where to take a question when the manual does not answer it.

  • UCLA Statistical Methods and Data Analytics Worked examples in R, Stata, SAS, SPSS, and Mplus. The first place I look for a syntax question.
  • Social Science Computing Cooperative University of Wisconsin-Madison. Public guides on Stata, R, and data management.
  • Statalist The Stata forum. Search it before asking, then ask well.
  • Quantitude Dr. Patrick Curran and Dr. Greg Hancock. A podcast on quantitative methods and the culture around them.

한국어 자료

Korean-language methods material, for students working in Korean or with Korean data.

  • 한국사회과학자료원 (KOSSDA) Seoul National University. Korea Social Science Data Archive.
  • Must Learning with R DoublekPark. A full R course in Korean, free on WikiDocs.
  • jamovi 튜토리얼 설현수 교수, 중앙대학교
  • StataEdu Stata training and materials in Korean
  • 교육통계연구소 Naver blog on education statistics and analysis
  • 고려대학교 교육연구소 방법론 워크숍 Workshop announcements from the Korea University Institute of Educational Research
  • R을 이용한 데이터 분석 Daum cafe. A long-running Korean R community.
